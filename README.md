# Formula-Student-Chassis-FEA
AISI 4130 spaceframe chassis for a Formula Student car, with FEA for frontal impact, rear impact and torsional stiffness (29.3 kg, FOS 1.70).
Design and structural validation of the AISI 4130 chromoly spaceframe chassis for UVEGA Motorsports, UVCE's first Formula Student team (targeting Formula Bharat and Supra SAE 2027).

Material: AISI 4130 steel Tools: SolidWorks, SolidWorks Simulation, ANSYS Mechanical

Show Image

What I did
Designed the spaceframe over multiple prototypes, cutting chassis mass from 35 kg to 29.3 kg (-16.3%) while keeping the safety margins.
Ran FEA on the load cases that matter: frontal impact, rear impact, and torsional stiffness.
Fixed an overstressed front hoop found during frontal impact analysis.
Cross-checked SolidWorks against ANSYS. The two disagreed by about 20% in stress. I traced it to a connectivity defect in the shared topology rather than a real difference in the physics.
Results
Check	Result
Chassis mass	29.3 kg (down from 35 kg)
Frontal impact (95 kN, per Formula Bharat T3.18)	FOS 1.70
Rear impact	FOS 2.56
Torsional stiffness	3,500.86 Nm/deg
